### Industry News

#### End-to-End Pretraining and Infrastructure Optimization for MAI-Base-2

**Company:** microsoft_ai  
**Industry:** Tech

Microsoft AI developed MAI-Base-2, an 80-billion-active-parameter, 2.8-trillion-total-parameter mixture-of-experts foundation model, by jointly optimizing data pipelines, model architecture, training algorithms, evaluation, and GPU infrastructure. The model was trained on 16,000 GB200 GPUs for 38 days, with reported 89% goodput and 21% average model-FLOPs utilization. Microsoft reports lower bits-per-byte than the open-weight models it tested on held-out code, math, STEM, and question-answering evaluations, and estimates a 4.2–12.3x pretraining-FLOPs efficiency advantage over DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base under its scaling-law methodology. The work is primarily a frontier-model research and production-training case study rather than a deployed end-user application: MAI-Base-2 was still undergoing post-training, although Microsoft stated that the model was intended for future Foundry licensing and use in Microsoft products.
